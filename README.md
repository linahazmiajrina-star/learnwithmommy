# Learn with Mommy · 学华文

Flashcards and quizzes for **SRJKC Bahasa Cina Tahun 1 (一年级华文)**, built from the free pinyin + English edition at
[multilingual-malaysian.github.io/SRJKC_resources/year1](https://multilingual-malaysian.github.io/SRJKC_resources/year1/#pinyin).

- **闪卡 Cards** – 274 words from all 22 chapters. Each card shows the word in 田字格 boxes (pinyin and picture optional).
  Tap to flip for pinyin, English and a sentence from the textbook. 🔊 reads it aloud. Mark words "I know it" to hide them.
- **测验 Quiz** – 5, 10 or 20 questions from the chapters you pick. Question types: meaning, pinyin, picture → word,
  listen → word, fill in the blank (sentences from the book), and next line of the Tang poems (古诗).
  Mistakes can be practised again as flashcards.

Progress (known words, best scores) is saved in the browser only.

## Play it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages: **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch**, choose this branch and `/ (root)`.
